# CompareX — supplier outreach kit (housing)

Prepared: 2026-10-25

## Goal and positioning

CompareX is an early-stage comparison and student-services packaging platform, starting with accommodation in Vienna. Users should be able to compare suitable offers and eventually arrange multiple student services through one CompareX journey. We are exploring partnership options and do **not** claim to have existing supplier integrations or booking volume.

Be transparent about stage and volume. Ask suppliers which commercial model is available now, rather than claiming eligibility for an API or single checkout.

## 4. Questions to ask on the first call

1. Which inventory and prices may we display, and how frequently are they refreshed?
2. Is API/feed access available to a startup at our stage? What minimum volume, company details or fees apply?
3. Can we initiate bookings and retrieve confirmed status, cancellations and amendments?
4. Does the customer have to complete checkout on your platform?
5. Can CompareX collect payment for the accommodation, or must payment go directly to you?
6. Who is the contracting party and merchant of record? Who handles refunds, disputes and chargebacks?
7. Are commissions, net rates or transparent markups permitted?
8. May the offer be bundled with unrelated services such as mobile plans, transport or gym membership?
9. Is a sandbox available, and what are the technical launch and security requirements?
10. What is the next step, and who will own the commercial and technical discussion?

## 5. Before sending

- Replace all bracketed fields and use a real business email.
- Link only to a public prototype that loads reliably; don't claim the supplier API is already connected.
- State current stage and realistic launch expectations; do not invent bookings, traffic or partner agreements.
- Ask for explicit written permission for any embedded booking, package resale, combined checkout or payment collection.
- Keep a record of contact date, person, reply, approved capabilities and next action in the CompareX supplier tracker.
